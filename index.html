About

Organisations rarely fail on strategy. They fail on the machinery.

The governance nobody owns, the process nobody wrote down, the change nobody sustained after the consultants left. That machinery is what I build.

I go end to end. Diagnose with evidence, not opinion. Design the operating model, the policy, the lifecycle and the governance. Build the capability and the tooling so the client can run it without me. And increasingly, put AI into that machinery where it earns its place, governed, banded and costed.

That covers transformation programmes, PMO and portfolio functions, policy and SOP architecture, enterprise Agile at scale, CMMI and maturity uplift, Lean Six Sigma and reusable AI accelerators, across banking, development finance, technology and manufacturing.

AI-Driven Transformation

From My AI Corner

Plenty of people claim AI skills that mean “I use ChatGPT.” My approach is to place every AI idea on a cost-of-ownership spectrum, band it, cost it, govern it, and build at the light-to-middle end myself.

The 6-band AI opportunity spectrum

Cost of ownership rises roughly an order of magnitude every two bands, about 300× end to end. The band, not the idea, sets the commitment.

1
Prompt-levelA person and a chat windowHours
2
Skills & ProjectsReusable instructionsDays
3
Copilot agentsAn agent on a licensed platformWeeks
4
RAG applicationsRetrieval over own documentsMonths
5
Custom platformsOwn app, database, SSOQuarters
6
Embedded & edge MLModels on operational streams~18 months
Light end returns hoursBuilt at bands 1 to 4 · prototyped at 5 to 6Heavy end returns client revenue
